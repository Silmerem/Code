Economy 1

The shipping question behind the diplomatic one.

  • Strait of Hormuz tensions linger as Iran and the US move further from a deal
    Al Jazeera27 Sep

    The economics do not reset when a proposal is refused; they compound. Every week the strait stays shut adds to insurance rates, to the tonnage rerouted through longer voyages, and to the share of Gulf crude that has to move by ship-to-ship transfer. A rejected seven-day plan is not a return to the status quo but another week of the same accumulation.

One headline. Nothing was published by a statistical agency on a Sunday, and the trade story of the week — the two-month US-China truce extension — ran on Friday.
