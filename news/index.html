Story of the day

Americans vote tomorrow and the oil price is already trading the result. Crude fell more than 1% this morning as Iranian and Qatari officials held a third consecutive day of talks on ending the war, with Brent near $111.84 after closing October at a six-month high. The president has said he is waiting for the midterms before ordering new action against Iran, which means the market is pricing a military decision against an electoral calendar. One analyst quoted this morning thinks the rhetoric resumes afterwards and takes Brent towards $120. Nobody is pricing the possibility that the talks work.
