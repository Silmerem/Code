Story of the day

Saudi Arabia has postponed its flagship investor conference to 2027. The Future Investment Initiative — the event the kingdom built to prove it is more than an oil exporter — was due in Riyadh next week with Jamie Dimon, David Solomon, Stephen Schwarzman, Larry Fink and Jane Fraser on the speaker list. It has been called off because the airport those guests would have flown into is closed and government staff in the capital have been told to work from home. A missile campaign run from the poorest country in the region has shut down the showcase for the richest one’s entire economic plan, and that is a more consequential result than anything either side has claimed on a battlefield this month.

Economy 2

A war has cancelled the meeting at which the money was going to be raised.

  • Saudi Arabia cancels the 2026 ‘Davos in the Desert’ amid Houthi attacks
    Al Jazeera20 OctToday

    The purpose of the conference is to attract the billions needed to move the Saudi economy off hydrocarbons, which makes its cancellation by an attack on hydrocarbon infrastructure a closed loop. The World Economic Forum postponed its own Gulf meeting in Jeddah in March for the same reason. The kingdom is now two years into a diversification programme it cannot hold a conference about.

  • Saudi Arabia postpones its big investor conference to 2027 as Houthi attacks on the kingdom escalate
    CNBC19 Oct

    Announced on Monday for an event due on 26 October, with new dates promised “very soon”. Dan Yergin calls the decision reasonable, which it plainly is — the first duty of a host is to be able to guarantee arrival. The cost is measured in deals not discussed rather than in anything that will show up in a quarterly figure.

Iran War 4

    Al Jazeera19 Oct

    Meetings with Araghchi and President Pezeshkian, with Moscow presenting the trip as an attempt to prevent a return to full-scale war and linking restored navigation to guarantees against further attacks on Iran. Araghchi blames the insecurity around Hormuz on American and Israeli military action.
