  • How the weak jobs report could make inflation harder to manage
    CNN Business8 Aug

    The trap for Kevin Warsh as he takes over the Fed: July’s job losses argue for easing, inflation near 3.5% argues for the opposite, and tariffs plus war-driven energy costs are pushing on the price side rather than the demand side. Cutting into a supply shock is how the 1970s went wrong, which is why the market is pricing hold rather than cut.

UK Politics 3

Clacton votes on Thursday with the major parties refusing to field anyone, and the polling that made Burnham look safe a week ago has stopped saying so. All three dates are inferred, not published — see the note below.

  • Andy Burnham set for crunch cross-party talks to end Britain's care crisis – but snubs Nigel Farage
    GB News6 Augest.

    Reform UK was left out of Thursday’s talks despite polling level with Labour, after Farage attacked the plans as a revived “death tax”. Excluding the opposition you are closest to in the polls is a choice about legitimacy as much as about care funding.

  • The inconvenient truth behind 'Burnham's bounce' — what the numbers really mean for Nigel Farage
    GB News5 Augest.Analysis

    Sir John Curtice’s reading of the two polls below, and the useful corrective to both. His argument is that a new leader’s honeymoon shows up first in personal ratings and only later, if at all, in vote share — and that on vote share Labour and Reform have not actually separated.

  • Labour wipes out Reform UK poll lead as party enjoys 'Burnham bounce'
    Yahoo News UK3 Augest.

    More in Common had Labour on 28%, up four in a week and four clear of Reform — against 20% and eight points behind a month earlier. Within days YouGov had Reform back in front by one. Both cannot be right about the level, which is Curtice’s point above.

The by-election in Clacton is on Thursday, and no reachable outlet has filed anything about it inside the window that can be dated — so it is not in the list above, only here. Labour, the Conservatives, the Liberal Democrats, the Greens and Restore Britain are all declining to stand against Farage, leaving 34 candidates and no major-party opponent.

Iran War 6

The deal with Oman is drafted and the price of using it has gone up: Tehran now wants the blockade lifted, the sanctions gone, the fleet withdrawn and compensation paid.

  • Iran war live: Pezeshkian seeks end to 'no war, no peace' amid Hormuz talks
    Al JazeeraToday9 AugLive blog

    This morning’s rolling thread. Pezeshkian’s framing is the interesting part: he is arguing publicly for an end to the frozen state the war has settled into, which is a different position from the security council’s list of preconditions.

  • Live updates: Tehran insists on US concessions before opening Strait of Hormuz
    CNNToday9 AugLive blog

    The US-side thread, and the detail worth watching in it: the chairman of the Joint Chiefs, Gen. Dan Caine, has been telling colleagues privately that Washington needs an off-ramp. That is a long way from the president’s public position that the strait opens or Iran gets hit hard.

  • UAE condemns Iranian attack on ADNOC tanker in Hormuz strait
    Al Arabiya8 Aug

    A missile struck a vessel belonging to Abu Dhabi’s state oil company as it crossed the strait on Saturday. No casualties, but the target matters: the UAE is not a belligerent, and hitting its shipping cuts against the argument that Iran can be trusted to run a transit corridor.

  • Iran deliberates Hormuz arrangement amid uncertain prospects with US
    Al Jazeera8 Aug

    Foreign minister Abbas Araghchi calls the Oman agreement “very close” while saying reopening depends on the US honouring a June memorandum Tehran says it already violated. The deal and the reopening are being deliberately kept as two separate things.

  • Iran demands concessions from US as it nears Strait of Hormuz deal with Oman
    CNN8 AugLive blog

    Saturday’s thread, where the demands were first set out in full. Also carries the Saudi warning that Riyadh is braced for “multiple coordinated attacks” from the Houthis and Iraqi militias — the second front that produced Friday’s defence pact.

  • Iran makes new strait demands, the UAE says a ship was targeted and other Middle East news
    NBC News8 Aug

    The list itself, which is longer than “lift the blockade”: never threaten Iran again, withdraw all military assets from the region, lift sanctions, unfreeze assets and compensate Iran for war damage. Read as an opening position it is negotiable; read as a precondition it is not a deal the US has ever signalled it would take.
