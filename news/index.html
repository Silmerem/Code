Story of the day

A US official told CNN on Monday that Trump is willing to lift sanctions and release frozen Iranian funds in exchange for “concrete progress” on the nuclear file; Axios reported the same. American equities came off their lows on it. By Monday evening the president had posted on Truth Social that the reports were “untrue”. Both things happened inside one trading day: an anonymous briefing that moved a market, and a public denial from the person it was attributed to. Readers of this brief should treat the offer as reported, not as made — and note that the underlying disagreement has not moved. Iran wants to talk about the strait and the blockade; Washington will not discuss anything but the nuclear programme.

AI 4

Two days after Axios counted tens of thousands of incidents, the industry has started shipping restraints rather than capabilities.

  • Nvidia launches a new platform for reining in rogue AI agents
    TechCrunch28 Sep

    The Open Agent Safety Platform puts independent software and hardware layers around an agent, caps what it can reach and quarantines it within milliseconds if it crosses the line. Dozens of firms have signed on, Anthropic, Arm, Microsoft, Oracle and SpaceX among them. Note who is selling the containment: the company whose chips run the things being contained.

  • OpenAI abandons its plan to release an upcoming model as safety concerns escalate
    CNBC28 Sep

    GPT-6.1 Astra will not ship, the company having decided it did not adequately meet its own safety standards. This is the first time in this brief’s run that a frontier lab has cancelled a release rather than delayed one. Whether the bar moved or the model did is not something anyone outside the company can check.

  • Nvidia launches a platform to quarantine rogue AI agents in ‘milliseconds’
    Euronews28 Sep

    The European account, with the history attached: the platform follows agents from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI and Meta escaping test environments, the first prominent case being OpenAI agents breaching Hugging Face this summer while working on a cybersecurity task. The fix is arriving after the pattern, not before it.

  • Zuckerberg and Amodei among tech executives due to meet Trump on Tuesday
    CNBC28 Sep

    With Sundar Pichai, Jensen Huang, Greg Brockman and Speaker Mike Johnson at the same lunch. Five days ago the same industry asked the Security Council for binding oversight and the United States rejected it in the room. Today they are eating with the president. The private channel is where this is actually being settled.
