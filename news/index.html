Story of the day

The economic war became a shooting war again on Sunday. US forces struck two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island, in the Strait of Hormuz, after observing crews preparing to fire rockets carrying mines into the shipping lanes — the first American strike on Iran in more than a month, and days after Central Command said it had finished clearing the last of the mines. Iran answered overnight with ballistic missiles at the US-operated King Hussein and Al-Azraq airbases in Jordan, which its foreign ministry says were used to mount the attack. Ten days of sanctions diplomacy undone in a single afternoon.

Iran War 4

A strike, a retaliation across a border, and a supreme leader who spent the same day asking the Gulf whose side it is on.

  • Iran says it attacked bases in Jordan and the UAE after the US strike on Larak Island
    Al JazeeraToday31 AugLive blog

    Ballistic missiles at the King Hussein and Al-Azraq airbases, aimed according to Iranian state media at technical infrastructure, maintenance facilities and fighter positions. The significant part is the geography: Iran has struck US installations on Jordanian soil, which makes a third country’s territory the site of the retaliation.

  • US strikes Iranian rocket launchers, marking the first attack in a month
    CNN30 Aug

    The stated justification is narrow and specific: crews on Larak Island were seen preparing to fire rockets carrying mines into the strait’s shipping lanes. It lands awkwardly beside last week’s announcement that Central Command had finished clearing mines from the international routes — and beside the Iran–Oman project to clear them jointly.

  • US forces strike Iranian rocket launchers as the IRGC warns of a 'retaliatory response'
    Euronews30 Aug

    The Revolutionary Guards confirmed the strike, reported casualties and promised an answer — delivered within hours. Worth noting how compressed that cycle has become: in February this war ran on days of deliberation between moves. It now runs on hours.

  • Iran's Khamenei calls for Gulf unity as Hormuz tensions persist
    Al Jazeera30 Aug

    A message for the Prophet’s birthday urging Gulf leaders to identify their “real enemy” — addressed to states Iran threatened a week ago and one of which, the UAE, cut all trade with it. In the same piece: annual inflation at 66% last month, and the US barring the UAE branches of Egypt’s Banque Misr from dollar transactions over alleged Iranian dealings.
