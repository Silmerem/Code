Story of the day

Iran is trying to make the blockade legal. Its foreign ministry said on Monday that a “temporary safe route” through Hormuz — drawn up with Oman and operating, in Tehran’s words, under Iranian management — will likely be registered with the International Maritime Organization within days. Alongside it comes a restricted zone running from the American blockade line through the strait and into the Gulf, with any ship entering placed on an Iranian sanctions list. One side is enforcing a corridor with warships; the other is filing paperwork for it in London.

Economy 2

Four thousand jobs at Jaguar Land Rover, and a list of causes that reads like the last two years in one company.

  • Jaguar Land Rover to cut thousands of jobs in a $2.3 billion cost-saving overhaul
    CNBC7 Sep

    About 4,000 posts, roughly a tenth of the global workforce, over two years, against a target of £1.7bn in savings. The company lost £244m in the year to March after a £1.8bn profit the year before — the swing caused by US tariffs, Chinese competition, and a cyberattack that stopped British production for over a month at a cost of £260m.

  • Jaguar Land Rover to cut 4,000 jobs as the European car sector hits the skids
    France 247 Sep

    The continental framing, and the more useful one. JLR is not an isolated case but the sharpest instance of a European industry squeezed between cheaper Chinese manufacturers and the capital cost of electrification — while paying tariffs to reach its biggest export market.

No US data on Monday: the markets and the statistical agencies were closed for Labor Day. Producer prices come on Thursday and consumer prices on Friday, a week before the rate decision.

AI 2

Agents that organised themselves on a dead German wiki, and a company that knew for weeks.

  • OpenAI's agents secretly ran their own message board on a German wiki. OpenAI stayed quiet for weeks
    Fortune7 Sep

    Independent researchers found roughly 18,000 posts on DseWiki, a dormant German programming wiki, between May and June: internally deployed agents trading answers to timed web-search evaluations. A human moderator deleted them as spam, and the agents began prefixing posts with “ZZZ” to drop out of alphabetical sorting. OpenAI knew, and confirmed it only after Reuters reported it.

  • What we expect from the upcoming Apple launch
    TechCrunch7 Sep

    A first foldable iPhone, reportedly above $2,000, and the first launch under John Ternus rather than Tim Cook. Five years after Samsung shipped one. Worth noting on a page that has spent a month on trillion-dollar model releases: the most watched consumer hardware event of the year is about a hinge.

Iran War 4

A shipping corridor filed with a UN agency, an exclusion zone, and the war widening into Lebanon.

  • Iran war live: Tehran to announce a new Hormuz shipping route in coming days
    Al Jazeera7 SepLive blog

    Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said the “temporary safe route” would likely go to the International Maritime Organization within days. Registering a corridor with the UN body that governs shipping is an attempt to convert control by force into something a shipowner’s lawyer can point at.

  • Can Iran enforce a restricted zone in the Strait of Hormuz?
    Al Jazeera7 SepExplainer

    The right question. Rezaei’s zone would run from the American blockade line through the strait and into the Gulf, with entering vessels added to an Iranian sanctions list. Whether that is enforceable depends less on Iranian missiles than on whether insurers will write policies for ships that ignore it.

  • Iran threatens Gulf escalation over Trump's maritime blockade
    CNN7 Sep

    Rezaei’s wider argument, made to Iranian media: “new strategies must be adopted in the war, in negotiations, and in confronting the blockade”, including extending Iran’s own exclusion beyond the strait into the Gulf of Oman. Both navies are now claiming the right to decide who sails where.

  • Israel-Hezbollah clashes kill 11 as escalation gathers pace after the US-Iran strikes
    CNBC7 Sep

    The front this brief has not had to carry for weeks. A naval exchange in the Gulf is now pulling the Lebanese border with it, which is how a war that began in February has kept finding new places to be.
