Story of the day

The hottest job in artificial intelligence this week is counterintelligence: former intelligence officers are reportedly being paid $500,000 to protect trade secrets. Three weeks ago the industry’s binding constraint was electricity. Then it was planning permission. Since Thursday it has been whether the people inside these companies can be trusted — the chief executive of the largest chipmaker resigned over an affair with a woman named as the founder of a firm reportedly investigated for smuggling his own chips to China. The report says Anthropic, which makes the model writing this sentence, has gone from fourteen counterintelligence staff last year to thirty-three this year. It prices its share sale on Wednesday.
