Iran War 2

Six dead on a cargo ship, fourteen vessels through a strait that used to take 130, and four days left on the memorandum that is holding the whole thing together.

  • Six killed in Houthi attack on Bab al-Mandeb ship, Yemen's government says
    Al Jazeera12 Aug

    Three ballistic missiles at a commercial vessel, fired twice at the same target. Three Pakistani nationals and one Indonesian among the dead, per Yemen’s transport ministry — the first shipping fatalities of the war, and they fell on the crew of a merchant ship belonging to none of the belligerents.

  • Strait of Hormuz traffic remains low as world rapidly burns through oil stockpiles
    CNN12 AugLive blog

    Fourteen crossings on Tuesday against about 130 a day before the war, and eleven of the fourteen took the Iranian-approved route the US has warned ships away from. The IEA’s warning is the part with a clock on it: stockpiles are being drawn down faster than the diplomacy is moving.

The June memorandum brokered by Pakistan and Qatar — the thing that ended open hostilities and started the talks — expires on Sunday. Pakistan’s interior minister was in Tehran on Tuesday trying to restart negotiations before it does; three of the six killed at Bab al-Mandeb were Pakistani.
