Economy 2

Wholesale prices did not move at all in July, and a September rate rise is quietly sliding into October.

  • Wholesale prices were flat in July, below expectations for 0.2% increase
    CNBC13 Aug

    Headline PPI unchanged against a forecast rise, core up 0.2% against 0.3% expected. Both undershoots, and coming a day after consumer prices matched forecasts, they remove the argument that pipeline pressure is about to resurface.

  • Producer Price Index shows another cool July inflation reading
    Axios13 Aug

    The number that keeps this from being a celebration: wholesale prices are still up 4.7% over twelve months, 4.2% at core. Traders now put a September hike at 42% and have moved their bets to October or December — delay, not reversal.

UK Politics 4

Farage is back in the Commons with a bigger share and a smaller mandate, and the count itself became part of the story.

  • Nigel Farage declares 'fairly convincing' victory in Clacton by-election
    ITV NewsToday14 Aug

    About 65% of the vote, which Reform had set itself as the bar for a big win, and more raw votes than he claims to have taken in 2024. Turnout was 44.37% — some 34,000 voters, more than fourteen points below the general election.

  • Nigel Farage claims by-election win but shuns count after 'advice from police'
    ITV NewsToday14 Aug

    The winner did not attend his own declaration, citing police advice. For a campaign built on confronting the establishment in person, staying away from the count is a detail worth more than the margin.

  • Nigel Farage wins Clacton by-election despite 'organised campaign to disrupt count'
    GB NewsToday14 Augest.

    GB News reports an organised attempt to disrupt the count itself. Treat the characterisation as the outlet’s until a returning officer’s account exists — but it is the same night, the same building and the same police advice that kept the winner away.

  • Nigel Farage declares victory against Count Binface in Clacton by-election
    Yahoo News UKToday14 Augest.

    The framing Farage will least enjoy, and the one the record will keep: he beat a man in a bin. With Labour, the Conservatives, the Liberal Democrats and the Greens all refusing to stand, a satirical candidate was the runner-up by default.

Two of these four carry dates verified against the URL, both from ITV News. The GB News and Yahoo lines are marked est. because those outlets publish no date — though for once the inference is not much of a stretch: the count concluded in the early hours of this morning, and neither story could exist before it.

Iran War 2

Two days until the memorandum expires, and the two sides cannot agree on whether the strait is even open.

  • US-Iran talks in deadlock — what's the latest?
    Al Jazeera13 Aug

    Tehran says it is not negotiating with Washington at all, only with Oman, which it wants as co-manager of the strait. Araghchi’s warning to the US to “be careful” over the control claim is the sharpest language from the foreign ministry this month.

  • Strait of Hormuz traffic remains low as world rapidly burns through oil stockpiles
    CNN13 AugLive blog

    The IEA’s warning is the one with a clock on it: reserves are being drawn down faster than the diplomacy is moving. Analysts credit Chinese stockpiles and higher US production for keeping prices as low as they are — neither is a permanent cushion.

The June memorandum brokered by Pakistan and Qatar expires on Sunday. Pakistan’s foreign ministry is calling it “the template of peace”; its interior minister was in Tehran this week trying to get talks restarted before the document runs out.
