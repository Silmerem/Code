Story of the day

It has a name now: a tanker war. Having lost three crude carriers on Saturday, Iran says its Guards struck three oil tankers and three US-linked vessels that evening, then attacked an American naval drone approaching the strait on Sunday morning. Tehran is preparing to declare a restricted zone outside Hormuz reaching into the Gulf. Brent opened this week at $97.13 — the highest of the war — and the targets are now the ships that carry the oil rather than the batteries that threaten them.

Iran War 4

Six vessels claimed in one evening, a drone attacked at dawn, and a new exclusion zone coming.

  • US and Iran are engaged in a tanker war. Where is the months-long conflict headed?
    Al Jazeera6 SepAnalysis

    The clearest account of what changed on Saturday. Both sides have moved from military targets to the commercial fleet, which is a category shift: warships can absorb losses, tankers are owned by companies and insured by markets. The escalation ladder now runs through the shipping industry rather than around it.

  • Iranian forces target a US naval drone attempting to enter the Strait of Hormuz
    Euronews6 Sep

    Sunday morning, an unmanned surface vessel off Iran’s south coast as it approached the waterway. Also carried: Admiral Cooper’s full warning, which went further than the line quoted everywhere — the US will, if necessary, “destroy Iran’s limited and exposed oil fleet”.

  • Iran threatens greater force if the US launches more attacks
    France 246 Sep

    The Guards described their Saturday targets as three tankers on an “unauthorised route” through the strait and three vessels affiliated with America. “Unauthorised route” is the phrase to watch: Iran is asserting a right to license passage, and enforcing it against ships that have not asked.

  • Iran touts its military response as the US maintains the blockade and hits tankers
    Al Jazeera6 Sep

    Mohsen Rezaei says a restricted zone outside Hormuz, extending into the Gulf, will be announced within days. If it is enforced the way the strait has been, the closed area grows beyond the chokepoint — and the number of neutral ships that have to choose between two navies grows with it.
