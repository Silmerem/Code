Economy 3

Inflation at 3.4% and falling, wages at 3.2% and falling faster: prices are cooling into a labour market that is cooling more.

  • US annual inflation cooled to 3.4% in July as gas prices ease
    CNN BusinessToday12 Aug

    The driver was energy: petrol and grocery prices fell on the month as the Hormuz panic premium partially unwound. Which is also the caveat — the improvement leans on an oil market that moved 5% the other way on Monday alone.

  • Inflation eases in July as gasoline and grocery prices fall
    NPRToday12 Aug

    The household read: the two prices people actually see weekly both fell. After 63 months above target — Warsh’s “unfair burden” line to Congress — a benign month is relief, not victory.

  • Here's the inflation breakdown for July 2026 — in one chart
    CNBCToday12 Aug

    Core at 2.5% annual is the number closest to target in the whole release. The gap between core and headline is almost entirely war-priced energy — which the Fed cannot fix by holding, hiking or cutting.

AI 2

Forty-eight hours after Meta, Nvidia opened a model of its own — the American open-weight counterattack is now a bloc, not a gesture.

  • Meta and Nvidia plant 'very firm flag' in open-weight AI race led by Chinese labs
    CNBCToday12 Aug

    Nvidia followed Meta’s Glimmer with Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, an open agent-focused model that runs on a single GPU. Both launch into a market where the popular open models are Chinese — Moonshot, DeepSeek, Qwen — and both still have to prove anyone switches.

  • Zuckerberg envisions 'superintelligence' for everyone in AI manifesto
    France 2411 Aug

    The argument underneath the releases: distribute the capability rather than centralise it, framed explicitly as the American answer to Chinese open source. The safety objection, from Future of Life’s Anthony Aguirre: nobody has explained how anyone controls what they hand out.

Iran War 4

The MoU’s 60-day clock runs out on Sunday. Pakistan says a deal is close; the US fired on a cargo ship; eight vessels crossed a strait that used to carry 130 a day.

    Two things in one thread: US missiles fired at a cargo ship accused of running the blockade, and Pakistan’s interior minister in Tehran trying to restart talks before the June memorandum’s 60-day negotiation window closes on Sunday.   • Iran refutes Trump's claim to 'control' Hormuz: What's the latest in talks?
