Finance 3

This morning’s trade was cheaper oil and calmer nerves; by the afternoon crude had reversed and equities went to a record anyway. Both halves are below, in the order they happened.

  • Oil rebounds as markets trade mixed following Wall Street rally
    EuronewsToday4 Aug

    Brent for October delivery gained 2.82% to $86.13 on supply worries, undoing much of Monday’s slide. Asian markets were mixed and the Nikkei slipped 0.3%, so the rebound was about the strait rather than any broad risk-off.

  • US stocks near record high, oil falls as Trump claims Iran talks under way
    Al JazeeraToday4 Aug

    The morning snapshot, written before crude turned. Worth reading against the story above: the same de-escalation narrative that knocked oil down at the open had stopped working by the afternoon, while equities kept climbing.

  • S&P 500 hits record, Dow surges 900 points after Bessent says Hormuz deal is near
    CNBC3 AugLive blog

    The rolling thread covering today’s session, opened last night. The S&P gained 1.8% for its first record since June, the Nasdaq 2.5% and the Dow more than 1,000 points, led by Caterpillar up 6%. US markets had not closed when this brief was built.

Economy 2

The yen intervention is holding, barely. Nothing else cleared the window today, so this section is carried over from the midday edition.

  • Fast and furious intervention to prop up the yen — will it work?
    CNBCToday4 Aug

    The intervention lifted the yen roughly 5%, from just above 163 to around 157 per dollar, and it has drifted back to 157.51 since. The test of a currency action is whether it survives the second week, not the first day.

  • Japan and US confirm rare joint intervention to prop up yen
    Al Jazeera3 Aug

    The first coordinated Tokyo–Washington action since 2011, after the yen hit a four-decade low of 164. Both treasuries said they would not hesitate to go in again.

June’s JOLTS openings were due today and July payrolls land on Friday, but no free coverage of the JOLTS release had surfaced by this edition.

AI 2

The frontier labs sat down with the White House today, and the framework they were shown is narrower than the rhetoric around it suggested.

  • Palantir stock rises 16% on soaring commercial revenue and AI sovereignty
    CNBCToday4 Aug

    Revenue grew 93% to $1.94bn, with commercial up 149% to $764m and government up 90% to $809m. The pitch is “AI sovereignty” — companies paying to keep their data away from the frontier labs, which is a bet against the labs the White House was meeting today.

  • White House to meet OpenAI, Anthropic and other top AI companies in first big regulation push
    CNN Business3 Aug

    OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta attended. The framework gives the government up to 30 days of early access to frontier models, and explicitly cannot be turned into mandatory licensing or preclearance — the opposite of the EU regime that became enforceable on Sunday.

UK Politics 3

Labour has erased Reform’s poll lead in Burnham’s first fortnight, with Farage fighting a funding scandal and the Clacton by-election he triggered still to come. These are not from the last 24 hours — see the note below the section.

  • Labour overtake Reform in major Westminster poll for first time in 16 months
    Yahoo News UK28 Julest.

    More in Common put Labour on 28%, up four in a week and four clear of Reform on 24%, with the Conservatives unchanged on 22%. Fieldwork ran 24–27 July across 2,061 voters — the first major poll to show Labour ahead since April 2025.

  • Reform's poll lead collapses amid row over Nigel Farage's finances
    Yahoo News UK28 Julest.

    The other half of the same swing: Reform stalled as Farage struggled to explain a £5m gift from Thailand-based crypto billionaire Christopher Harborne. The undeclared donations row is what pushed him to resign his Clacton seat and force a by-election he now contests against 33 rivals.

  • Burnham set for cross-party talks on the care crisis — but snubs Farage
    GB News28 Julest.

    Invitations went to Kemi Badenoch and Ed Davey but not to Reform, despite its polling. Farage called it “the uniparty in action” and accused Burnham of “conspiring behind closed doors with the failed political establishment”.

Iran War 5

The first official confirmation that something real is happening — from Rubio, about talks with Oman rather than Tehran — landed on the same day a ship was abandoned in the strait those talks are meant to reopen.

  • Rubio says progress made in Strait of Hormuz talks after Trump threatens ‘decapitation’
    CBS NewsToday4 AugLive blog

    The Secretary of State said progress had been made between Iran and Oman, “but not finality yet” — the first time an official has put a name to the negotiation Trump has been asserting for two days. Note what it is not: talks between Washington and Tehran.

  • Trump and Iran clash over whether talks exist as a cargo ship is attacked in Hormuz
    NBC NewsToday4 Aug

    A dry bulk carrier was struck by an unknown projectile off Oman; the crew abandoned ship and one seafarer is missing. Traffic through the strait is still a trickle of its peacetime volume, which is why the oil price moved on a single vessel.

  • Iran war live: Tehran slams US blockade as Trump warns of ‘last chance’
    Al JazeeraToday4 AugLive blog

    Today’s rolling thread, running Tehran’s complaint about the naval blockade alongside the denial that any bilateral talks exist.

  • Trump warns Iran facing ‘last chance before decapitation’
    Euronews3 Aug

    The language that set up today. Trump framed the talks as a final opportunity to “sign a good document” while insisting they were already under way.

  • Trump blasts 'duplicitous' Iran after Tehran denies it is in talks with the US
    Al Jazeera3 Aug

    Iran’s foreign ministry said its negotiations were with Oman over safe passage, not with Washington. Rubio’s statement today reads as a quiet concession that Tehran’s version was the accurate one.
