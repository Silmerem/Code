Story of the day

Masoud Pezeshkian addressed the United Nations while his country is at war with the country hosting it — something no leader has done in person before. He opened by holding up photographs: children killed in the strike on the Shajarat al-Tayyiba school in Minab on the war’s first day, in which 156 people died, and Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, killed in the same opening salvo. “Yesterday the United States president described us as terrorists. We have been the victims of terrorism.” The offer inside the defiance was explicit: ready for dialogue, but not “the language of force”, and no nuclear weapons but no limits on peaceful nuclear technology either. Two days after the first direct contact since February, both governments have now stated their terms in public from the same rostrum.

Finance 4

The biggest day in the ten-year Treasury in a year and a half, and it had nothing to do with the Gulf.

  • Ten-year Treasury yield rockets to a 19-year high. Here’s what’s driving the spike
    CNBC23 Sep

    To 5.135%, the highest since July 2007, in the largest single-day move for nearly eighteen months. Three causes, none of them Iran: activity surveys far stronger than forecast, hawkish remarks from a Federal Reserve governor, and a five-year note auction that found poor demand. The last of those is the one to watch, because it is about who will buy the debt rather than what the economy is doing.

  • Ten-year Treasury yield hits 5.1% for the first time in 19 years
    CNN23 Sep

    The same move from the other side of the trade. A yield at this level reprices mortgages, corporate borrowing and the federal interest bill together, and it has now been reached twice in ten days — the second time without an oil shock to blame it on.

  • Stock futures flat after soaring Treasury yields trigger a market sell-off
    CNBC23 SepLive updates

    The S&P 500 fell 0.75% to 7,706.03, the Nasdaq 1.13% to 26,936.04 and the Dow 352.10 points to 51,511.59 — Monday’s record given back inside two sessions. Oil went the other way: Brent rose 3.86% to $103.08 after five days of falls, with US crude up 1.81% at $92.16.

  • Nscale’s $35bn IPO is heavily dependent on ByteDance’s access to Nvidia’s chips
    Fortune23 Sep

    Two days after the filing showed a $1.02bn loss on $140.6m of revenue, the dependency underneath it: contracted work with Microsoft worth $43.8bn to December 2033 and with Anthropic worth $44.6bn, in a business whose economics turn on whether a Chinese customer can keep buying American chips. That is export-control policy priced as an equity.

Iran War 4
