Equities took the de-escalation trade and ran with it this morning; the bond market was less convinced, and Tehran's denial arrived after the rally. Everything here is a bet on an Iran headline that did not hold.

    CNBCLive blog

    The Dow traded around 613 points higher (1.2%), the S&P 500 up 1.5% and the Nasdaq up 2.2%. Meta rose almost 7% and Amazon gained more than 6% to touch a $3 trillion market capitalisation.

  • What's moving markets today
    CNN BusinessLive

    CNN's rolling markets thread — the place to check whether the afternoon reversal in the Iran story pulled equities back before the close.

  • US Treasury yields fall as oil prices plunge on Iran de-escalation hopes
    CNBC3 Aug

    CNBC31 Jul

    Brent settled at $90.12 on Friday after the IRGC said it hit tankers moving through Hormuz under US naval escort, with four more turning back. That is the level today's slide started from.

Economy 5

Growth is slowing while inflation refuses to come down, and manufacturers now describe the uncertainty as worse than the pandemic. Markets put a September Fed hike at 64.5% by midday today, down from about 82% in late July — entirely on the Iran headlines.

  • Federal Reserve holds interest rates steady despite high inflation
    NPR30 Jul

    The FOMC left its key rate at 3.5%–3.75% on a 9–3 vote, with three dissenters wanting a rise. Markets are trading as though higher rates are coming regardless.

  • Manufacturing survey shows inflation worries 'worse than pandemic era'
    CNBC3 Aug

    The ISM July survey came in at 55.6, the fastest expansion in more than four years, led by export orders and a 6.3-point jump in production. Manufacturers describe geopolitical uncertainty as worse than Covid.

  • Don't be fooled: America's inflation problems aren't going away anytime soon
    CNN Business13 Jul

    On sticky services inflation — haircuts, doctor visits, car repairs — which has now run above target for more than five years and is the part an oil-price reversal does not fix.

  • Eurozone economy grows despite Middle East war
    France 2430 Jul

    The bloc expanded 0.4% in Q2, double the 0.2% forecast, holding up better than expected against the energy shock. The ECB, on hold at 2.25%, faces its own September question.

  • US GDP growth dips as inflation and trade deficits pressure economy
    Al Jazeera30 Jul

    Q2 growth cooled to 1.5% from 2.1% in Q1, with the PCE price index up 3.7% year on year in June and widening deficits subtracting from the headline.

AI 5

Brussels switched on real enforcement powers today, in the same week Chinese labs closed the gap on open models — regulation arriving just as the competitive picture stops being American.

  • OpenAI and Anthropic face new scrutiny as EU AI Act enforcement powers kick in
    CNBC3 Aug

    The Commission can now inspect models, demand evaluations before EU release, restrict market access and fine providers up to €15m or 3% of annual turnover. Brussels is already in contact with OpenAI and Anthropic after the recent model-driven cyberattacks.

  • Alibaba shares rally after unveiling its 'most powerful' AI model
    CNBC3 Aug

    Alibaba published benchmarks showing its new model scoring comparably to, and in places above, leading US frontier models. Separately today, Hugging Face's chief executive said Chinese labs could draw level with US model makers by early 2027.

  • The AI industry is picking sides on open or closed tech. Experts say follow the money
    NBC News

    Framing for the two stories above: Nvidia, Microsoft and Meta have lined up behind open-weight models, while OpenAI and Anthropic — the closed-weight leaders — stayed out of that alliance.

  • Nvidia and OpenAI in talks for up to $250 billion backstop to fund AI infrastructure
    CNBC27 Jul

    The arrangement would let OpenAI raise debt for a 10-gigawatt data centre campus in Pike County, Ohio, on the strength of Nvidia's credit — a chip supplier underwriting its own customer's borrowing.

  • Analysis: will Big Tech's colossal AI spending crush Europe's data sovereignty?
    EuronewsAnalysis

    Background to today's EU move. Morgan Stanley expects hyperscalers to borrow around $400bn in 2026, more than double 2025 — the scale of capital European regulators are now trying to set terms for.

UK Politics 5

Two weeks in, Burnham has bought goodwill with three cost-of-living giveaways. The bill for them, and the gilt market's opinion of it, is John Healey's problem — and Farage is already demanding a general election.

  • Burnham targets lower energy bills in first move as PM
    NBC News

    From 1 October, VAT comes off domestic electricity bills — about £45 a year for the average household — funded by scrapping Starmer's digital ID scheme, which would have cost £1.8bn.

  • Burnham pledges to end instability; Farage calls for a general election
    CNN20 Jul · Live

    Burnham promised his leadership would be a "circuit breaker" bringing "the biggest changes in the last 40 years". Farage's reply: he "cannot be allowed" to do that without a mandate.

  • Burnham offers sweeteners and hopeful vibes in first week as UK PM
    France 2426 Jul

    Alongside the VAT cut: a cap on most bus fares in England and a 20% cut in business rates for pubs, clubs and live music venues. Three modest, visible cost-of-living measures.

  • All eyes are on John Healey, the UK's new finance minister. Here's what's at stake
    CNBC21 Jul

    Healey inherits rising borrowing and a gilt market that has already shown it will react. The giveaways above are small; the question is what the autumn fiscal event looks like.

  • What to know about Andy Burnham, the new UK prime minister
    NPR19 Jul

    The primer, if you have not followed the leadership contest: how the Greater Manchester mayor took the party, and why Labour MPs concluded he was the one who could take on Reform.

Iran War 5

The day's whole story is a contradiction: Washington says negotiations have started, Tehran says they have not. What appears real is an Iran–Oman deal on Hormuz transit, said to be in its final stages.

  • Trump calls Iran 'unbelievably duplicitous' after Tehran denies his claim talks are resuming
    CBS News3 Aug · Live

    Trump said Iran asked for a meeting — "some would say beg" — then publicly denied any discussions were happening. He also said a plan was ready for "the biggest attack since World War II" and that he halted it for diplomacy.

  • Iran denies peace negotiations, contradicting Trump's announcement
    NPR3 Aug

    The cleanest write-up of the contradiction. Tehran says it is talking only to Oman, about temporarily reopening the Strait of Hormuz, and to no one in Washington.

  • Live updates: Iran discusses a Strait of Hormuz deal with Oman but denies talks planned with the US
    CNNLive

    The rolling thread, useful for tracking whether either account holds overnight. Trump has said Hormuz could open fully by Tuesday in what he calls "phase one".

  • Trump says new Iran negotiations are starting: what we know
    Al Jazeera3 Aug

    Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the Tehran–Oman negotiations on future management of the Strait are in their "final stages" — a temporary safe route, not a US agreement.

  • Iran says no current talks with the US
    Al Arabiya3 Aug

    Foreign ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei's statement, reported from the region — worth reading alongside the US coverage for how the denial is being framed locally.
