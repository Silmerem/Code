Story of the day

The war moved to the water. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard fired ballistic missiles at two American warships — an aircraft carrier and a guided-missile destroyer, both of which evaded them — and the United States answered by disabling two Iranian crude carriers and destroying a third. The doctrine was stated openly by the Central Command chief, Admiral Brad Cooper: “If you shoot at two of our ships, we will impose an even higher economic cost — taking out three of yours.” Tehran calls the tanker strikes a war crime and has claimed fresh attacks on shipping this morning.

Iran War 5

Missiles at two warships, three tankers destroyed or disabled, and a threat to a nuclear site.

  • Iran war live: the IRGC claims new attacks on three oil tankers and three US-linked ships
    Al JazeeraToday6 SepLive blog

    This morning’s thread. Tehran is answering the tanker strikes in the same currency, and the target set has widened from warships to commercial vessels with American links. If Saturday established an exchange rate for ships, Sunday is the first test of it.

  • US strikes three Iranian oil tankers after missile attacks on Navy ships
    CNBC5 Sep

    Central Command named the vessels: the crude carriers M/T Downy off Kharg Island and M/T Stark 1 near Jask permanently disabled, and the unladen M/T Kylo destroyed in the Gulf of Oman. Kharg is where Iran ships almost all its oil, so the strikes land on the export capacity that sanctions have been trying to close for a fortnight.

  • US military strikes three Iranian tankers in retaliation for missile attacks
    CNN5 Sep

    The sequence: the Revolutionary Guard fired at a carrier and a destroyer, both evaded, no Americans hurt, and the response came against economic rather than military targets. That is a deliberate choice, and Admiral Cooper explained the arithmetic behind it in public.

  • US military says it destroyed Iranian oil tankers after missile attacks
    France 245 Sep

    Note the phrasing throughout: this is Central Command’s account, and the ship names and outcomes come from it. Iranian state media confirmed a strike near Kharg separately. Independent verification of what happened in the Gulf of Oman is not yet available.

  • US says it hit Iranian oil tankers accused of helping finance 'regional proxies'
    Al Jazeera5 Sep

    Tehran’s side: the foreign ministry calls the strikes a war crime and the armed forces threaten more attacks on American vessels while the blockade holds. Also in the day’s reporting, Trump said the US may target Iran’s Pickaxe Mountain “very soon” — a nuclear site, and a category of target this war has so far avoided.
