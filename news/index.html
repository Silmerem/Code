Story of the day

Trump’s answer, relayed by a Fox correspondent, was “we’re going to hit them very hard”, and he posted an AI-generated video to Truth Social appearing to show Iran’s Kharg Island being bombed. Finance 2

August finished up, on a day when almost everything fell except the one sector the war pays.

  • Wall Street closes out a winning August on a losing day
    CNBC31 AugLive updates

    The Dow lost 374 points, or 0.7%, to 53,185.90; the S&P fell 0.33% and the Nasdaq 0.12%. For the month the S&P still rose 2.6%, the Nasdaq 3.9% and the Dow 1.3% — a fifth consecutive monthly advance. Nearly every sector fell on Monday. Energy was the exception.

  • Gas topped $4 a gallon daily in August for the first time — and new strikes are pushing oil higher again
    Fortune31 Aug

    Brent back above $90, US crude up more than 3%, Exxon up 2.9% and Chevron 3%. The $4 daily average is the number that will be quoted in American politics for the rest of the year: it is where a distant war becomes a domestic price, and it arrived before this weekend’s escalation.

Economy 2

A rate rise in two weeks is now the market's base case — into a war that has just restarted.

  • Markets see Warsh endorsing a September rate hike. Not everyone is convinced
    CNBC31 Aug

    Odds on a rise at the 15–16 September meeting moved up to roughly 60–66% on Monday depending on the gauge, close to double where they sat before Jackson Hole. The dissenting case is straightforward: Warsh described conditions as not broadly restrictive and declined to give guidance, and a market that reads a refusal to guide as a commitment may be hearing what it expects.

  • Treasury yields tick lower as the US-Iran war moves back into the spotlight
    CNBC31 Aug

    The 10-year at 4.712%, the 30-year at 5.21%, the 2-year at 4.327%, all marginally down. Note the direction: renewed war pushed money into government bonds even as it pushed oil up, which is the opposite of the pattern that has held all month. Two very different weeks are being priced at once.

The data that settles it lands this week: ISM manufacturing and JOLTS today, and August payrolls on Friday. July lost 23,000 jobs against an expected gain of 83,000.

AI 2

Nvidia buys another piece of the chain that might route around it, and the labs go looking for a story the public will like.

  • Nvidia's $3.5bn MediaTek bet reveals its plan for Big Tech's chip buildout
    TechCrunch31 Aug

    MediaTek will use Nvidia’s technology to design custom chips for AI companies and hyperscalers — chips that plug into Nvidia-based data centres. The strategy is now unmistakable, a fortnight after Hugging Face and Poolside: if customers are going to build their own silicon, Nvidia intends to be paid on that too.

  • AI giants like Anthropic bet on health care to fix the public backlash
    Axios31 Aug

    Dario Amodei promising “incredible results in the coming years and some early glimmers in the coming months” in health and biology, with an IPO approaching. Read it against his own diagnosis a fortnight ago: that the sharpest criticism of AI companies is that they have not delivered on their big promises. Curing disease is the promise; the glimmers are still pending.

Iran War 4

Two strikes, a promise of a third, and a tanker hit overnight.

  • Tanker struck in Hormuz, raising worries over escalation, as Trump vows to hit Iran 'hard'
    CNBCToday1 Sep

    This morning’s development, and the one that decides whether the exchange stops at two moves. A commercial vessel hit in the strait while Washington is publicly committed to a response leaves very little space between the two sides for anything other than another strike.

  • Iran attacks Jordan and the UAE after the US bombs Larak Island: what's the latest
    Al Jazeera31 Aug

    The IRGC called it “Punishment of the Aggressor” and said it hit technical infrastructure, maintenance facilities and fighter positions at King Hussein and Al-Azraq; Jordan says it intercepted eight missiles. Tehran’s justification is that the bases were used to mount the Larak strike — a doctrine that makes any host of American forces a target.

  • Trump threatens to 'hit Iran hard' as strikes resume after a month-long lull
    France 2431 Aug

    “There will be a response.” Also on Monday, an AI-generated video posted to Truth Social appearing to show Kharg Island under bombardment — Iran’s main oil export terminal, and not a target that has been struck. A fabricated image of an attack on the country’s oil lifeline is a threat issued in a new medium.

  • Iran launches missiles at bases in Jordan after the US attack
    CNN31 AugVideo

    Footage and the sequence laid out end to end. Worth two minutes if the last four days have been hard to follow: mines, a strike on the launchers, missiles into a third country, and a promise of more — all inside a week that began with a sanctions press conference.
