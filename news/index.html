Story of the day

The war reached Saudi Arabia’s refining industry at dawn on Sunday: a Houthi drone set fire to an Aramco refinery at Jazan, on the Red Sea coast. Nobody was hurt and the blaze was put out. On the same day the Pentagon told American arms manufacturers to raise output, because stockpiles are running low.

UK Politics 3

Clacton votes on Thursday, and the national polls have stopped agreeing with each other — one has Reform third, another has them first, inside the same week. All three dates are inferred, not published — see the note below.

  • The inconvenient truth behind 'Burnham's bounce' — what the numbers really mean for Nigel Farage
    GB News5 Augest.Analysis

    Sir John Curtice’s corrective to the two surveys below. A new leader’s honeymoon shows up first in personal ratings and only later, if at all, in vote share — and on vote share Labour and Reform have not reliably separated at all.

  • Nigel Farage's Reform drops to third in bombshell poll as Labour and Tories storm ahead
    GB News3 Augest.

    Lord Ashcroft has Reform on 18%, their worst since the 2024 election, behind Labour on 27% and the Conservatives on 23% — worth 16 seats on his projection. GB News reports Reform dismissing it as an outlier, and the article itself notes a YouGov poll released days later put them back in front. Reform’s average since Burnham took office is 24%.

  • Labour wipes out Reform UK poll lead as party enjoys 'Burnham bounce'
    Yahoo News UK3 Augest.

    More in Common, separately, had Labour on 28% and four clear. Two pollsters within days of each other agreeing Labour leads, a third days later saying the opposite, and a spread on Reform running from 18% to 24% — that is the state of the evidence going into Thursday.

The by-election itself is on Thursday and no reachable outlet has filed anything datable about it inside the window, so it stays here rather than in the list. Labour, the Conservatives, the Liberal Democrats, the Greens and Restore Britain are all declining to stand, leaving Farage facing 33 other candidates and no major-party opponent.

Iran War 5

Iran says it is not negotiating at all. Meanwhile the Houthis hit a Saudi refinery, and Washington quietly told its arms suppliers to make more.

  • Pentagon memo to defense companies, Houthi attack on Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery
    CNN9 AugLive blog

    The memo is the story inside the story: the Defense Department urging contractors to ramp up production because stockpiles are running low. It is the clearest signal yet that Washington is planning for a long war even while saying a deal is close.

  • Iran denies any direct talks with U.S. on opening Strait of Hormuz as Houthis claim attack on Saudi refinery
    CNBC9 Aug

    Tehran’s denial that talks are even happening sits awkwardly against Trump saying on Sunday that the US is “low-keying” negotiations. Oil read the denial rather than the claim and rose 1.2%.

  • Houthi attacks kill seven in Yemen, refinery targeted in Saudi Arabia
    Al Jazeera9 Aug

    The Houthis said the Jazan strike answered Saudi drones crossing into Yemeni airspace over Hajjah and Saada. Al Jazeera puts the death toll from the accompanying strikes inside Yemen at seven.

  • Yemen's Houthis attack kills 11 in Yemen, hit Saudi refinery
    Euronews9 Aug

    The same events counted differently: Euronews reports eleven dead where Al Jazeera reports seven. Both are same-day figures from a contested war zone, and neither is verifiable from here — which is why both are shown rather than one being picked.

  • Yemen's Houthis claim attack on Aramco oil facility in Saudi Arabia
    NPR9 Aug

    The wider regional round-up, including vice-president JD Vance’s line that the US aim is to “maximise the amount of oil and gas coming out of the Strait of Hormuz”. That is a narrower objective than the total reopening the president has been demanding in public.
