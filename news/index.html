Iran War 5

The route is agreed and the text is being drafted. What is not agreed is the one thing that makes it operative — whether the US lifts its blockade in exchange.

  • Iran says close to Hormuz plan with Oman, but reopening depends on US
    France 24Today6 Aug

    Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei says the agreement is “on the verge of being finalised” and a joint statement is being prepared covering technical, legal, security and environmental points. The condition attached: no reopening until the US naval blockade of Iranian ports ends.

  • Iran war live: Tehran, Oman close in on Hormuz deal; Houthis attack tankers
    Al JazeeraToday6 AugLive blog

    Today’s rolling thread. Houthi attacks on tankers running alongside the drafting is the reminder that a Hormuz agreement does not settle the Red Sea, which is a separate chokepoint with separate belligerents.

  • Iran and Oman agree route for ships in Strait of Hormuz, Tehran says
    Euronews5 Aug

    The concrete step: both sides finalised the geographical coordinates of the proposed shipping lanes on Wednesday. Coordinates are the part diplomats can settle; the fee and the blockade are the parts they cannot.

  • Iran, Oman, US 'close' to Hormuz deal: what do they all want?
    Al Jazeera5 Aug

    The explainer worth reading before the announcement lands: three parties with three different definitions of success, and an interim agreement that gives each of them something they can present as a win.

  • Trump says deal to reopen Strait of Hormuz could come as early as today
    NBC News5 Aug

    Yesterday’s prediction, which did not happen. Worth keeping on the page as a check on the next one: this is the fourth time in a week a deal has been called imminent from Washington.
