Story of the day

  • BRICS summit 2026: what are the key takeaways?
    Al Jazeera13 SepExplainer

    The New Delhi Declaration was adopted unanimously on day one despite splits over Iran and Ukraine.     The American read on the same document. What the bloc can actually do about a closed strait or a tariff war remains the open question — a unanimous declaration among members with directly opposed interests in the Gulf is easier to reach the less it commits anyone to.

AI 2

The labs have asked to be slowed down; Congress has about a week, and not everyone thinks the request is what it looks like.

  • Big AI sets out its terms for regulatory capture and calls it ‘Pace the frontier’
    The Register14 SepTodayAnalysis

    This morning’s counter-argument, and it is worth reading against the item below rather than before it. The Register’s case is that a rulebook written to the specifications of the three companies that would be regulated by it is a moat, not a brake — and that export bans and mandatory testing regimes happen to raise the cost of entry for everyone smaller. That is an argument, not a finding; so is the labs’ sincerity.

    CNBC13 Sep

    The specifics the piece above is arguing about, set out on Sunday: Dario Amodei wants Nvidia’s most advanced chips barred from sale to China, a crackdown on model distillation, and a national law requiring frontier models to be tested — and says Anthropic will give independent evaluators permanent, employee-level access inside the company. The timing problem is congressional: the Speaker is due to send the House home after this week until the November midterms.
